# Privacy policy

Effective date: 15 August 2026

## Summary

The extension reads the eBay UK Seller Hub order list you open so it can highlight orders that match your rules. For lists with several pages, it reads the other pages in the browser. It processes order details inside Chrome and does not send them to the developer or another service.

## Information the extension handles

The extension reads parts of the current Seller Hub order list needed for its features, including later pages of that list when pagination is present:

- Order totals and postage amounts
- Buyer names or account identifiers
- Order status and selection state

You choose thresholds, colours, page preferences and toolbar text. Chrome stores these settings.

## Use and storage

The extension uses order-page information to mark matching rows, show counts and select eBay order checkboxes when you request a bulk selection. It keeps this information in the open page and does not retain order details after the page closes.

Chrome stores extension settings through `chrome.storage.sync`. Chrome may sync those settings to browsers signed into your Google account. The developer cannot access your synced settings.

## Sharing and transfers

The developer runs no server, analytics, advertising or tracking for the extension and does not receive, sell or share order details or settings. Chrome can sync settings through your Google account. eBay and Chrome continue to handle information under their own privacy terms when you use those services.

The developer follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, for information received from Google APIs.

## Permissions

The extension requests access to eBay UK Seller Hub order pages so it can read and mark the current order list, including other pages of that list when needed. It requests browser storage so it can save your settings.

## Changes

The developer will update this policy and add a notice in the extension if these data practices change.

## Contact

Ask a privacy question through the [project support page](https://github.com/evenwebb/order-marks-downloads/issues). Do not include customer names, addresses or order numbers in a public issue.
