# Privacy policy

Effective date: 14 August 2026

## Summary

Order Marks reads the eBay UK Seller Hub order page you open so it can highlight orders that match your rules. It processes order details inside Chrome and does not send them to the developer or another service.

## Information the extension handles

The extension reads parts of the visible order table needed for its features, including order totals, postage amounts, buyer names or account identifiers, order status, and selection state. It also handles the settings you choose, such as thresholds, colours, page preferences, and toolbar text.

## Use and storage

Order-page information is used to mark matching rows, show counts, and select eBay order checkboxes when you request a bulk selection. This information remains in the open page and is not retained after the page closes.

Chrome stores extension settings through `chrome.storage.sync`. Chrome may sync those settings to browsers signed into your Google account. The developer cannot access your synced settings.

## Sharing and transfers

The extension has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The developer does not receive, sell, or share order details or extension settings. Chrome can sync settings through your Google account as described above. eBay and Chrome continue to handle information under their own privacy terms when you use those services.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Permissions

The extension requests access to eBay UK Seller Hub order pages so it can read and mark the visible order table. It requests browser storage so it can save your settings.

## Changes

Any update that changes these data practices will include an updated policy and a notice in the extension.

## Contact

Open a privacy question through the [Order Marks support page](https://github.com/evenwebb/order-marks-downloads/issues). Do not include customer names, addresses, order numbers, or other private information in a public issue.
