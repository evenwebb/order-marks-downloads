# Privacy policy

Effective date: 15 August 2026

## Summary

The extension reads the eBay UK Seller Hub order list you open so it can highlight orders that match your rules. If that list has more than one page, it also reads the other pages of the same list in the browser. It processes order details inside Chrome and does not send them to the developer or another service.

## Information the extension handles

The extension reads parts of the current Seller Hub order list needed for its features, including later pages of that list when pagination is present:

- Order totals and postage amounts
- Buyer names or account identifiers
- Order status and selection state

The extension also handles the settings you choose, such as thresholds, colours, page preferences and toolbar text.

## Use and storage

The extension uses order-page information to mark matching rows, show counts and select eBay order checkboxes when you request a bulk selection. It keeps this information in the open page and does not retain order details after the page closes.

Chrome stores extension settings through `chrome.storage.sync`. Chrome may sync those settings to browsers signed into your Google account. The developer cannot access your synced settings.

## Sharing and transfers

The extension has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising or tracking. The developer does not receive, sell or share order details or extension settings. Chrome can sync settings through your Google account as described above. eBay and Chrome continue to handle information under their own privacy terms when you use those services.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Permissions

The extension requests access to eBay UK Seller Hub order pages so it can read and mark the current order list, including other pages of that list when needed. It requests browser storage so it can save your settings.

## Changes

Any update that changes these data practices will include an updated policy and a notice in the extension.

## Contact

Open a privacy question through the [project support page](https://github.com/evenwebb/order-marks-downloads/issues). Do not include customer names, addresses or order numbers in a public issue.
